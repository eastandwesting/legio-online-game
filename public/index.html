HOW TO FIGHT

Controls

  • Left-click a regiment to select it; drag a box to select several; Shift adds to the selection.
  • Right-click an enemy: attack it. Right-click the ground: march there, keeping formation.
  • Right-drag: form a line along the drag, facing the enemy.
  • A select all · 1 swords · 2 spears · 3 archers · 4 cavalry
  • S stop · Esc deselect · H or ? this help
  • Unit cards along the bottom: click selects, Shift-click adds, double-click selects every regiment of that type.

Match-ups

  • Spears stop cavalry.
  • Cavalry runs down archers and wins on the flanks and rear.
  • Archers wear infantry down from range, until their arrows run out.
  • Rear attacks break morale: a regiment whose morale bar empties routs (white flag) and takes no more orders.

Time limit

A battle ends when one army breaks or when the clock runs out. At the time limit the defender holds in a campaign; otherwise the side with more of its army left wins.
